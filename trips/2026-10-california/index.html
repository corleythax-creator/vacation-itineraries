How these are figured

Straight up I-5, San Francisco to Los Angeles is about 380 miles. The coast route costs you roughly 265 extra, nearly all of it on the Oct 5 Big Sur leg.

Average mph is moving time, not elapsed — stops are taken out, or the sightseeing legs would read like walking pace. They are estimates for planning: 51 on the open 101, 37 through Big Sur because Highway 1 will not let you go faster, and the teens and twenties everywhere you are threading a city. Seventeen hours behind the wheel across five driving days.

Fuel: roughly 20¢ a mile, about $130 for the trip. California regular averaged $5.93 a gallon on Sept 11, 2026, and a non-hybrid midsize SUV returns about 30 mpg. If SIXT actually hands you a 2026 RAV4 — hybrid only now, 41 mpg combined — it drops to 14¢ a mile and about $95. Either way it is 16 to 22 gallons. Gas in Big Sur is scarce and priced accordingly, so fill up in Marina before you leave on Oct 5.

How these are figured

Speed is gate to gate, so it reads well below the roughly 500 mph these aircraft cruise at — taxi, climb and descent are in the number. It is also why the two Jackson hops look slow: 350 miles is barely enough to get up and back down.

Fuel is per seat, and estimated. The Houston legs are full narrowbodies on long sectors, which is the efficient case; the Jackson hops are 76-seat regional jets spending most of a short flight climbing, which is the worst case. Across all four legs it averages about 59 miles per gallon per seat, or roughly 63 gallons each — 190 gallons to move the three of you there and back. US airlines averaged 67 available seat-miles per gallon in 2024, so these numbers sit either side of the fleet.

Worth a look: the rental car does 90 passenger-miles per gallon with three of you aboard at 30 mpg — better, per person, than any leg of the flight. The whole 647-mile drive burns about 22 gallons. One Houston-to-San Francisco seat burns 23.

Typical October highs and lows. A live forecast from Open-Meteo replaces them here automatically once the trip falls inside the 16-day window — from about Sept 22. If the page cannot reach the network, the normals stay.

The spread is the thing to plan for: Solvang swings more than thirty degrees between afternoon and dawn, Anaheim stays hot into a party that runs to 11 PM, and the two coastal stops never get warm. Universal City sits in the Valley and runs hotter than either coast.

What to pack

For the swing

  • A light jacket or fleece for all three, every single day
  • Layers you can shed by noon once you turn inland
  • Long pants for coastal evenings — the wind off the water does the work, not the temperature

Park days, Oct 6 and 7

  • Broken-in shoes. Two park days back to back, and Oct 7 is when fatigue shows up
  • A battery pack and a cable each — wait times, tickets and photos all run off the phone
  • Sunscreen and hats for the Universal day, which is almost entirely outdoors
  • A dry shirt or a poncho. Jurassic World soaks you and you have a hotel to drive to
  • Costumes if you want them. Adults may dress up at the Bash, which is not true on an ordinary Disney day

The car and Big Sur

  • Snacks and full water bottles before Oct 5. Services are thin and gas is expensive
  • Something for motion sickness — Highway 1 is curves for two hours
  • Offline maps downloaded before you leave Carmel; cell service south of there is unreliable
  • A car charger, not just the wall one

Do not leave without

  • Jessica’s license and a credit card in her name. The rental is hers and the counter will not budge
  • All three Bash tickets loaded in the Disneyland app before you leave Solvang
  • IDs for the flight, and the chargers swept off the nightstand every morning

Worth knowing

  • Deliberately not on the plan: Alcatraz, Hearst Castle, Beverly Hills, Griffith Observatory, Venice and Malibu. Each one was cut so a day stays enjoyable instead of rushed.
  • The car is a SIXT midsize SUV — a RAV4 or similar, five seats and four bags. SFO pickup 10:30 PM Oct 2, LAX return 11:00 AM Oct 8. One-way drop, so confirm the drop fee is already in the rate.
  • Two park days back to back, both heavy walking, with a late night in between. Oct 7 is the day fatigue shows up.
  • Pebble Beach charges $12.50 per car for 17-Mile Drive, refundable against a $35 spend at a Pebble Beach Resorts restaurant.
  • Universal parking is separate from general admission. Download the Universal app the night before for wait times and showtimes.
  • Recheck before you go: Highway 1 south of Big Sur (closed for fire as of Sept 13, see Oct 5), Universal’s posted hours for Oct 7, and that all three Bash tickets are in the app.
  • Time zone: Pacific is two hours behind Jackson the whole trip. Mornings are the easy part — a 7:00 alarm is 9:00 at home. Nights are the cost: landing at 9:50 PM on Oct 2 is 11:50 on the body clock, and the party ending at 11:00 is 1:00 AM.
